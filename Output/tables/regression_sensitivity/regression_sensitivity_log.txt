4. Placebo windows
==============================================================================

  pseudo-window of the same length (21 trading days) starting at every position s
  of the sample such that the pseudo-window does not overlap ANY of the three

  and the pseudo-interaction coefficient, its HAC(5) t, HC3 t and classical t
